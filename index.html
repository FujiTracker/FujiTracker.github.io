Market price is the average of the cheapest ~15% of listed units, so one-off cheap or pricey listings don’t skew it. Click a row to see its reagents (each links to its auction page). Tick or untick “In total” to build your own set. Items with no auction listings (Crystal Force, Whipper Root Tuber, Blood of Heroes) are flagged and left out of the cost unless you enter a price for them in the row. Formula/recipe costs aren’t included. The SAF-T Ultra Precision Scope (Engineering) isn’t listed on the auction house, so it isn’t tracked. Prices refresh from the auction scan about every 30 minutes; this page re-checks every 5 minutes. Reagent counts from RankedBoost; enchant picks from the Forever Hunter guide.
